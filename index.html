About

Telecom is unforgiving. A missed OTP is a lost login and a dropped call is a lost customer. That is the standard I test against, every release, on real devices, in production conditions.

I am a QA lead based in Lahore, working on a VoIP and eSIM platform used by operators and their subscribers. My team owns quality across mobile apps, web portals and the APIs underneath them, from OTP flows and call routing to eSIM provisioning and analytics events.

Alongside the platform, I take on technical design and QA for national-scale work: a SIM registry for a West African regulator, an A2P SMS honeypot for fraud detection in the Sahel, and voice firewall acceptance for a Southeast Asian operator.

AI is a working tool here, not a headline. It drafts test cases from specs, generates edge-case data and triages logs faster than I can read them. The judgement about what matters stays with me.
